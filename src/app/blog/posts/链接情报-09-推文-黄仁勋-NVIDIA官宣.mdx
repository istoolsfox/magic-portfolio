# 黄仁勋官宣推文（全文）

> Today, with over 100 industry partners, we introduced the NVIDIA Open Agent Safety Platform, bringing together OpenShell and Sentry.
>
> Artificial intelligence is extraordinary technology that will advance discovery, productivity, security, health, and prosperity for all of humanity. But its power demands responsibility. As AI agents move from answering questions to acting on their own, they need to be monitored — the way that every human employee is.
>
> The Open Agent Safety Platform establishes open standards for verifying that agents stay within the boundaries their operators set, with control points on the path between agents and models. Built on the principles of verifiable policies, out-of-band enforcement, and shared responsibility, it's the trust layer for the agent economy.
>
> Thank you to our more than 100 partners for building this with us. Together, we're ensuring that the most transformative technology of our time is safe, secure, and accountable.

## 要点

- **100+ 产业伙伴**联合发布
- 核心比喻："AI agent 需要被监控——就像每个人类员工被监控一样"
- 定位：**agent 经济的信任层（trust layer for the agent economy）**
- 三原则关键词：verifiable policies（可验证策略）、out-of-band enforcement（带外执行）、shared responsibility（共享责任）、**control points on the path between agents and models**（agent 到模型路径上的控制点）
- 发布时机：Perplexity 红队报告（09-23，8/10 沙箱沦陷、OpenShell 唯二守住）之后 5 天
